Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers "Channel uploader", a Google Apps Script tool run by Built Wrong Labs. Only Built Wrong Labs uses it, to post videos to its own YouTube channels.

What it can reach

When you sign in, you let Channel uploader use your YouTube account through the YouTube Data API. It uses that to:

  • Read your channel's name, to make sure it posts to the right channel.
  • Upload videos, set their thumbnails, and add them to playlists.

It does not read your viewers' data, comments, messages, or anything outside YouTube.

What it keeps

It keeps the sign-in key Google gives it, so it can keep posting without asking you each time. That key is stored in the tool's private settings in Google Apps Script. It also keeps a list of the videos it posted (title, link and date) in our own GitHub repository.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data with anyone. We don't use it for ads.

Channel uploader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

You can remove Channel uploader's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. After that, it can't post anything.
